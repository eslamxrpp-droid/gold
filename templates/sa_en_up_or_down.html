What "up" and "down" mean here

We compare the current price with the most recent daily figure we hold, and we print its date next to the number. A move smaller than 0.05% counts as flat. If no earlier day is available we say so rather than quietly comparing against some other day.

This is a price comparison only. We do not forecast the gold price, explain why the market moved, or advise buying or selling.
