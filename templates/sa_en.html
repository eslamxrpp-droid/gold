FAQ

What is the difference between 24K, 22K and 21K gold?

Karat shows how much of the metal is pure gold: 24K is pure, 22K is 91.7% gold and 21K is 87.5% gold. In Saudi Arabia 21K is the most searched karat; 22K is common in Indian-style jewellery.

Why is the shop price higher than this rate?

Shops add a making charge per gram and a margin. When you sell used gold, shops usually pay less than the market rate.

How is the rate calculated?

We take the global price of one troy ounce of pure gold in US dollars, convert it at the fixed rate of 3.75 riyals per dollar, divide by 31.1035 grams, then multiply by karat ÷ 24.
